Old Mo has only this one portion of oil left, enough to keep the searchlight lit for a short while.

When things go wrong at the fog's most slippery foothold, it lets you light the lamp and steady yourself - but it can only be used once, and after that only an empty flask remains. When the investigation roll lands on 96-100, panic may also burn it all up.
