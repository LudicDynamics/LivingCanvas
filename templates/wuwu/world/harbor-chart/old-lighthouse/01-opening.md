The lighthouse keeper Old Mo says that on that night he put out the light for twelve minutes. Why exactly during that stretch of time?

Reading the logbook or asking Old Mo requires no dice roll. To pin the thread here, you need Old Mo's telescope to look at the Old Customs Tunnel mouth - the result determines whether you see the tunnel mouth clearly, only get the tide table, or drop and break the telescope. Old Mo has only one portion of lamp oil to lend: the decisive check on the chart requires standing at the fog's most slippery foothold, and carrying it will make things much steadier.
