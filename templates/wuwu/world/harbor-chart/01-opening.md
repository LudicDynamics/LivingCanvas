Go to Berth 7 first and look at the scars left on the ship. At the Workshop you can investigate the ship's parts, and at the Lighthouse you can investigate the time the lamp went out. The order is up to you.

Reading clues, questioning people and laying out materials cost no tide and need no dice. Each place also has one investigation dice card, which can be rolled only once: the result decides whether that thread is pinned on the spot. On a success the evidence goes into your bag; on a failure you get only Half a Thread, or someone is alarmed, and you must take the Long Way Around to pin it, spending more tide. The Harbor Map itself holds the riskiest roll of all, the decisive check: find the Second Wake in the fog. Borrow some lamp oil from the Old Lighthouse first if you can.

Once all four threads are pinned, make your accusation on the Accuse card. If you haven't accused anyone by the time the tide returns, the second ship will vanish into the fog.
