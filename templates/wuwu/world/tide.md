The tide is at its lowest, and fog clings to the water. Old Mo says that once the tide returns, the second ship can slip quietly out of the fog.

It is eleven at night. The tide will return around one in the morning. Every time you roll an investigation die, the tide rises a notch: a dozen or so minutes pass (except on a critical success, when the search is especially quick); alarming someone costs even more; and any thread you miss means taking the Long Way Around, another notch. Before the tide comes in, pin every thread to the Accuse card on the Harbor Map.
