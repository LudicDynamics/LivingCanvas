The tide has returned. The water at the Old Customs Tunnel's mouth rises past the marker line; a very soft sound of oars comes through the fog, then nothing more is heard.

The clues in your hands are still scattered across the chart: the unmanned ship, the twelve dark minutes, the clasp with the Lord's crest. They could have joined into one line, but the second ship has already sailed away with the secret in the Lord's ledger.

Silver Kite still watches the empty ship at Berth 7, and Old Mo lights the lighthouse as usual. The harbor will say it was only fog; only you know that what was missing was time.
