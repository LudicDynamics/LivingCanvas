The outermost foothold slips, and the fog closes in from all sides. You reach for lamp oil. There is not a drop of burnable oil at hand, no light.

By the time Old Mo finds you with a lamp, you are clutching a soaked mooring post, and the second ship's wake has long been smoothed away by the tide.

The threads you found are still in your bag: the decoy ship, the Old Customs Tunnel, the Lord's ledger. But the Accusation was never made, and the truth of that night stays in the fog.
