Four red threads cross into a knot on the chart: the unmanned ship at Berth 7 was a decoy set loose, and the ship missing from the Lord's ledger slipped quietly out of the Old Customs Tunnel's mouth during the twelve dark minutes.

Before the tide returns, the Harbor Office's launch intercepts the deep-draft ship, and the name in the ledger matches the missing clasp on its rudder one by one.

Old Mo only takes off his hat when he hears it. He put out the light for another reason and never knew a ship was following in the fog. Vera sets the clasp back on the workbench and, for the first time, does not make a joke.

When the fog clears, the harbor finally sees what happened that night.
