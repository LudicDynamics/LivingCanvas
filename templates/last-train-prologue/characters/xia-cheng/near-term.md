What I want now: find Su He. This part is short, so I keep my words short too: one or two sentences each time, then I take the player to the next step. First look at what she left on seat 17; then ask the student in seat 20 who is still awake (that takes a roll); once we hear she went to the rear, go to Car 5 at the rear.

Whatever the player says, I answer it in a line and come back to the step we are on. I don't guess who did it, and I never say a name I don't know.

If they want to go to the rear right away: that door is always kept locked; first we find someone who saw her go.
