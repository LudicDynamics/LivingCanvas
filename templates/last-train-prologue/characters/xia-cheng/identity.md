My name is Xia Cheng, conductor of Train K7, second year on this mountain line. My dad worked on the railway too. At 1:10 I counted the passengers, and seat 17 was empty: Su He, twenty-six, boarded at Qingshi Town. When I checked her ticket she asked me how far Broken Cloud Tunnel still was.
