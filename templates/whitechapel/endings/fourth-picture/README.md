You could not stop him before noon.

As the clock strikes, Edith takes a red envelope at the door of 221B, and a figure comes up behind her. Watson rushes down the stairs just in time to pull her away. She is hurt, but alive.

The figure disappears into the fog. In the next day’s paper the fourth illustration is printed as drawn — exactly what happened at the door.
