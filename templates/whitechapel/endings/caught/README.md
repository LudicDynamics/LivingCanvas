You lay the evidence out in front of Wayne, piece by piece: the illustration that arrived before the incident, the ultramarine on the railing at the scene, the fresh injury on his wrist, and the correction he signed for at 8:15.

Wayne looks at it for a long time, then lets go of the red envelope. Inside is the proof of the fourth picture — and the person taking the envelope in it has Edith’s face.

As Lestrade’s men lead him away, the clock strikes noon. Nothing happens.

Edith finishes the fourth chapter in the sitting room at 221B. This time, she lets no one read it first.
