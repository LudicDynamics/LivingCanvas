Noon strikes, and nothing happens on Baker Street. Edith is safe; Watson never leaves her side.

But Wayne walks away. He tucks the red envelope back into his coat and takes his leave politely. What you hold is enough to make people wonder, not enough for Lestrade to hold him.

The fourth picture did not come true. You know who staged the first three — you just could not say it in front of everyone yet.
