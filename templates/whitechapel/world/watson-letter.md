Holmes, I need your help. Three times now, incidents straight out of a novel by my patient Edith have really happened. The next is set for noon today.

She’s afraid people are being hurt because of her. Find out who read the manuscript first, and stop the next incident. She is under protection in the guest parlor at 221B. Please don’t tell anyone where she is without her permission.
