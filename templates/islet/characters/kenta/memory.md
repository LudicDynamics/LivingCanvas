portable: false

The old boat's keel is sound; half the planks are rotten. There isn't enough timber on the island, but driftwood off the tideline will do once it's dry. The newcomer stood on the dock a long time today, looking at the boat.

What I do not know, I do not say. I add no secrets from scenes I did not see.
