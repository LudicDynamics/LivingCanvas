You walk back into the night library with the name of your spell still warm on your tongue. Behind you, in the stores, the space it opened is still there — it did not close when you looked away.

Seraphina has kept your seat at the reading table. She asks you to say the name once more, and writes it in the margin of her own notebook, beside the price you agreed to pay.

“Most first spells open a drawer,” she says. “Yours opened somewhere you can walk into. Remember what it cost, and the next one will be yours too.”

The librarian enters it in her ledger as if it were a book: borrowed tonight, to be returned whenever you are ready.
