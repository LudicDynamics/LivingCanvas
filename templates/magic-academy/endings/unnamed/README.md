You put the materials back where you found them: the star fragment still warm, the mirror reflecting only candlelight, the two words never spoken aloud.

Seraphina does not stop you. She folds your draft once and tucks it into the back of her notebook. “A spell you didn’t cast hasn’t cost you anything,” she says. “It just hasn’t happened yet.”

The librarian marks nothing as borrowed tonight. On your way out you look down at your name tag; the line is still blank.

Tomorrow night the library opens again at the same hour. The materials will be where you left them.
