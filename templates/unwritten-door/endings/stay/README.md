You close the door and draw the latch. Whatever is outside can stay unwritten. Iris looks at you for a long time, then pulls out the other chair and makes tea in two chipped mugs, as if she knows where everything is kept.

The rain doesn't stop. It doesn't get worse either. You talk until the lamp burns low, and each time it does, someone has already trimmed the wick.

Nights pass, or one night keeps passing. The letter stays where you left it. Her envelope stays sealed.

One evening, across the way, a lamp comes on behind a door you have never seen open. Iris stands up. "I'll go," she says. "Someone just woke up." You realise you have been here long enough to be the one who is used to the sentence.
