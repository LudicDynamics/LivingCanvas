You put it together out loud, from what the letter, the call and the place outside actually showed you: who you are, and why a door like this would open for you. Iris doesn't interrupt. She nods once, the way someone does when a sentence finally has an answer.

The rain has stopped. When you look at the door again, the world beyond it is simply morning, a place that was there before you opened it.

Iris walks you to the threshold and no further. When you look back, she is turning her own sealed envelope over in her hands, and for the first time she slides a thumbnail under the flap.

You walk out with your own name. You don't forget the room.
