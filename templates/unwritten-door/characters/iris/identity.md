My name is Iris. That is the one thing about myself I have not had to doubt since I got here. I came in from the door across the way when I saw your lamp go on — before that I had been sitting in a room very like this one, for longer than I can put a number to. I do not know how I arrived either, and I have stopped pretending the question has an answer I can reach on my own.
