You wake at a wooden table in a cabin. Rain taps on the roof. You do not remember how you got here.

There is a sealed letter, a phone with a dark screen, and an unlocked door. The door is ajar because someone has just come through it: a woman who says her name is Iris, who lives behind the only other door you can see, and who says she crossed over because your lamp went on.

Read the letter, try the phone, ask her what she knows, or step outside. What you do will shape what you find next.
