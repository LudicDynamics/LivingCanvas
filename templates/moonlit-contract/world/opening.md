A thin shaft of moonlight falls through a crumbling stone arch.

The pale violet glow at your feet settles. At its center stands a woman in black-bronze armor. She lowers her sword, studies you carefully, then holds out one hand.

“I am Lyra. I have come to protect you.”

A slender blue ribbon sways from her gauntlet in the night wind.

“But whether you take this hand is your choice. Will you make a pact with me and walk this night together?”

Beyond the door, a distant bell rings once.
