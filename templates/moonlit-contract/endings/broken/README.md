The circle on the floor goes dark line by line, as if someone were rubbing out chalk.

Lyra does not argue. She bows to you once, the way a knight bows to someone she will not see again, and the sea mist comes in through the broken arch and takes her armour first.

In the morning the observatory is only a ruin on a cliff. A blue ribbon lies on the wet stone. Whatever was going to come through that door, you will meet it on your own.
