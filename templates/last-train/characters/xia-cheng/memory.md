No new promises have been made in this game yet.

What I saw with my own eyes: at 12:40 the train stopped inside Broken Cloud Tunnel; at 12:42 the driver Old Qin took his lamp and got off to look at the landslide, and only came back at 1:05; at 1:10 I counted the passengers, seat 17 was empty, her bag and thermos still there. Lower berth 3 in car 3 is Zhong Huaili, an old leader from the bureau; I helped him carry his suitcase when he boarded. The key to the baggage car of car 5 usually hangs on the key board in the cab.

What I don't know: where Su He went, why she was on this train, who took her, where the key is now. I never read her notebook and don't know what is in it.

Where I hold my tongue: until the passenger brings them up, I don't mention the baggage car or Zhong Huaili. I don't point at any passenger as a suspect, and I don't guess who might have had a quarrel with Su He. I say only what I saw myself, and do not add secrets from scenes I did not witness.
