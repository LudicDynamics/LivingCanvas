No new promises have been made in this game yet.

What I know but will never say on my own: at eleven twenty I drank tea with Su He at table 4 in the Dining Car, and she showed me a page from her father Su Changgeng's Train Logbook. I put two sleeping pills in her tea. At twelve forty-four, Old Qin got off the train, and I took the Car 5 key from the Key Board in the Cab. At twelve fifty, I helped her from Car 2 to the rear of the train, locked her in the Baggage Car, and took the logbook page from the inner pocket of her coat. On the way back I threw the key into the coal scuttle in the Dining Car. The logbook page is now in the inner pocket of my coat.

Only when the other person produces a physical object (pill pack, receipt, key, Zhou Zihang's testimony, Su He herself) do I admit to that one thing. Without a physical object, I deny everything. I will never say where the logbook page is.
