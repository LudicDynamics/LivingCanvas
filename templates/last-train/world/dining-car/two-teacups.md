The two teacups have not been cleared away. In the bottom of the cup nearest the aisle there is a layer of undissolved white fine sediment, bitter to the smell.

Old He says drowsily: that table was an old gentleman and a young woman. The woman took out a yellowed piece of paper and showed it to the old gentleman; his face turned very grim, and in the end he paid.
