The last page reads: "Tonight he's on the train. Zhong Huaili. Twelve years, and I've carried Dad's logbook with me the whole time. When we reach Lingkou, I want him to admit it in front of everyone."

Tucked inside is an old newspaper clipping: in November twelve years ago, two trains collided in Broken Cloud Tunnel, three people died, and signalman Su Changgeng was found to have been derelict in his duty.
