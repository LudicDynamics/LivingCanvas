portable: true

Su He huddles in the mail sacks, shivering, her voice very soft:

"A bit past eleven... I found him in the dining car. I showed him my father's logbook. He said, 'Have some tea, we'll talk slowly.' The tea was very bitter... after I got back to my seat I could not stand steady. Someone helped me walk toward the rear, and their coat smelled of camphor. The logbook... the logbook was in my coat's inside pocket."

She touches the inside pocket - it is empty.
